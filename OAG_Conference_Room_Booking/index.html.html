Conference room guidelines

  • Please leave the room clean and tidy after use.
  • Bookings should be made only for official OAG business.
  • Please cancel the booking if the room is no longer required.
  • Do not exceed the maximum room capacity of 20 people.
  • Ensure all equipment is switched off after the meeting.

Help

Use the conference room booking system to select a room, choose an available date and time, and submit your meeting details.

  • Select the required conference or meeting room.
  • Select an available date from the calendar.
  • Select a starting and ending time. Bookings can be made in 30-minute increments.
  • Complete all required meeting details.
  • Select the number of attendees according to the room capacity.
  • Click Book to submit the booking.
  • Use your booking reference and email address to manage or cancel a booking.
